# Citing Spectypus

Use this reference, replacing the version and commit with those used in your analysis:

> Spectypus: supernova spectral classification software. GitHub:
> https://github.com/sPaMFouR/spectypus (version X, commit Y).

Find these with `spectypus --version` and `git rev-parse HEAD` in the source
checkout used for the analysis. For older runs, use their recorded provenance.

Also cite the classifiers that contributed to your results:

- **SNID-SAGE:** [Blondin & Tonry (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1086/520494)
  and [Stoppa & Smartt (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stag1066).
- **NGSF:** [Goldwasser et al. (2022), TNS AstroNote 2022-191](https://www.wis-tns.org/node/138742)
  and [Howell et al. (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1086/497119).

Copy entries from [references.bib](references.bib), and cite the sources of
the observed spectra and template collections used in your analysis.
